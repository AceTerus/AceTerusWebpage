About AceTerus

AceTerus is a free, AI-powered learning platform built for Malaysian secondary and pre-university students preparing for SPM, PT3, STPM, and university coursework. The purpose of AceTerus is to help Malaysian students study more effectively by combining AI-generated quizzes, an AI study companion, and social learning tools into a single web application.

AceTerus is owned and operated by the AceTerus team. The official website of AceTerus is https://aceterus.com.

What AceTerus does

AceTerus provides Malaysian students with a modern set of study tools in one place. Students can generate quizzes from their own notes or textbook PDFs, practise with objective and subjective questions, receive instant AI grading and feedback, and track their progress through streaks, achievements, and performance analytics.

  • AI-generated quizzes from text or PDF uploads, tailored to Malaysian syllabuses (SPM, PT3, STPM).
  • OMR scanning of physical answer sheets for instant grading of paper-based quizzes.
  • Ace mascot — an AI study companion that answers questions in English or Bahasa Malaysia.
  • Subjective answer grading and personalised performance analysis powered by AI.
  • Study tools: Pomodoro timer, materials library, goals, reminders, and streak tracking.
  • Social feed: posts, comments, likes, and direct messaging between students.
  • Gamification: ACE Coins virtual currency, achievements, and Boss Raid challenges.
  • Events platform: student event registration, reward codes, and deals.

How AceTerus uses Google Sign-In

AceTerus uses Google Sign-In to let students create and access their AceTerus account without managing a separate password. When you sign in with Google, AceTerus requests only your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture). AceTerus does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service.

The information AceTerus receives from Google is used solely to create and identify your AceTerus account. For full details on how AceTerus handles your data, please read the AceTerus Privacy Policy.

Who AceTerus is for

AceTerus is designed for Malaysian students aged 13 and above who want a modern, engaging way to prepare for major examinations and daily coursework. AceTerus is used by SPM and PT3 candidates, matriculation and STPM students, and university undergraduates across Malaysia.
