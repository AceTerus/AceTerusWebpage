AceTerus

AceTerus is a free, AI-powered learning platform built for Malaysian secondary and pre-university students preparing for SPM, PT3, STPM, and university coursework.

What AceTerus does

AceTerus helps students study more effectively by combining AI-generated quizzes, an AI study companion, and social learning tools into a single platform. Students can generate quizzes from their own notes or textbook PDFs, practise with objective and subjective questions, receive instant AI grading and feedback, and track their progress through streaks, achievements, and performance analytics.

Key features of AceTerus

  • AI-generated quizzes from text or PDF uploads, tailored to Malaysian syllabuses.
  • OMR (optical mark recognition) scanning of physical answer sheets for instant grading.
  • Ace mascot — an AI study companion that answers questions in English or Bahasa Malaysia.
  • Subjective answer grading and personalised performance analysis powered by AI.
  • Study tools: Pomodoro timer, materials library, goals, reminders, and streaks.
  • Social feed: posts, comments, likes, and direct messaging between students.
  • Gamification: ACE Coins, achievements, and Boss Raid challenges.
  • Events platform: registration, reward codes, and deals for student events.

Who AceTerus is for

AceTerus is designed for Malaysian students aged 13 and above who want a modern, engaging way to prepare for major examinations and daily coursework. It is used by SPM and PT3 candidates, matriculation and STPM students, and university undergraduates.
